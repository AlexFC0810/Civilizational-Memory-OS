## Public FAQ

### Was 'A'isha nine?

The dominant Sunni report says nine at consummation, but the Qur'an does not mention her age and serious historical research disputes whether the report preserves reliable chronology. A precise alternative age is not proven.

### Are Bukhari and Muslim wrong?

A canonical report can meet traditional hadith criteria while a historian still questions when and why its numerical content emerged. These are different methods. Muslims need not treat every historical detail in a hadith collection as revelation.

### Did Islam invent child marriage?

No. Minor marriage existed in many premodern societies before Islam. Muslim jurists nevertheless regulated and sometimes reinforced it, so pre-existence is context—not absolution.

### Does Qur'an 65:4 permit child marriage?

Classical exegetes often understood one category as girls too young to menstruate. The verse regulates a divorce waiting period. It does not command child marriage, establish nine as an age, or forbid later protective legislation.

### Is age eighteen Islamic?

Eighteen is not a revealed numeral. It can be an Islamic policy boundary designed to protect consent, maturity, health, education, and equal access to law.

### Are you condemning the Prophet?

No. The exact age is historically uncertain, and even accepting the traditional report would not make one event a universal command or block present child-protection rules.

### Why not just say she was eighteen?

Because the evidence does not securely prove it. Islam does not need apologetic invention.

### What is the strongest Qur'an-first standard?

Adult capacity, meaningful consent, sound judgment, comprehension of the solemn covenant, physical and psychological readiness, and protection from coercion and harm.
