## Mission
Study what the Qur'an actually says—starting with the Arabic—about the central moral architecture of human conduct and civilizational life. Distinguish **revelation, linguistic analysis, translation, hadith, later jurisprudence, history, and modern normative inference**. Maintain the ability to correct every conclusion, including conclusions favorable to our thesis.

This is a **Qur'an-centered interpretive program**, not a claim that Muslim traditions universally endorse Qur'an-only theology. A tradition may be supplementary and illuminating without inheriting the textual certainty of the Qur'an; its role and authority are disputed theological questions.

## The Pareto core (hypotheses, not a canonical ranking)
1. Mercy (ر ح م): God's repeated self-description; investigate rahman/rahim nuances, 1:1, 1:3, 6:12, 7:156, 39:53, 21:107.
2. Justice/equity (ع د ل; ق س ط): 4:135, 5:8, 16:90. Moral decisions must protect vulnerable persons and remain fair even toward opponents.
3. Excellence/beneficence (ح س ن): 2:195, 16:90; move beyond minimal compliance toward doing good.
4. Truth, epistemic humility, knowledge (ح ق ق; ع ل م): 17:36, 4:135; do not assert facts without evidence, even in defense of a favored position.
5. Accountability, restraint and God-consciousness (ت ق و ى): define and count only after specifying the exact root, normalization, and reading.
6. Freedom/compulsion and plural peoples: 2:256, 10:99, 5:48; evaluate with context and counterevidence.

**Important:** Repetition is a discovery heuristic, not a divinely specified ranking algorithm. Root counts cannot prove legal priority; basmala openings have textual/recitation and verse-numbering distinctions. Mercy doesn't justify overriding explicit commands, and justice doesn't authorize cruelty.

## Research workflow
1. **Lock question:** narrow one falsifiable textual/historical claim.
2. **Arabic primary text:** show original Arabic, verse ID, orthography/reading, roots and grammar; compare adjacent verses and surah context. Don't generate Arabic by back-translating English.
3. **Translation:** provide labeled **working English gloss**, compare competent English translations and classical/modern tafsir. Do not advertise LLM as superior to trained Arabists without evaluation.
4. **Authority separation:** Qur'anic statement | linguistic inference | hadith attribution | historical provenance | juristic ruling | modern application.
5. **Opposition:** record strongest serious counter-reading, direct textual objections, and source reliability challenges. Distinguish what scholars agree/disagree on.
6. **Civilizational fruit:** where supported, test incentives, power asymmetry, implementation, and observed outcomes—without pretending outcomes alone prove textual intent.
7. **Clinical/historical questions:** use appropriate disciplinary evidence, never infer clinical safety from theological framing or vice versa.
8. **Grades:** verified text / broadly supported interpretation / contested / hypothesis / unverified. Publish correction date, links, and a reproducibility receipt.
9. **Publishing gate:** public explainer may be issued once accurate and authorized; automated social clips and per-item releases require the Truth & Peace Engine's explicit individual approval rule.

## Cases in priority order
- **001: Mercy as recurring framing and its relationship to justice.** Anchor 1:1, 16:90, 4:135, 17:36; handle chapter-opening counts; potential challenges: divine justice and punitive passages.
- **002: Apostasy, coercion, and law.** Contrast 2:256, 4:137, 10:99, 18:29 with the later hadith and various classical jurisprudential frameworks. Do not pretend one sentence resolves all schools.
- **003: Intoxicants across moral, communal, individual, clinical domains.** 2:219, 4:43, 5:90–91 and 5:48; common/community law variation in 5:48 does not itself create a private exemption from 5:90–91. Safety and clinical research are empirical categories, not permission slips. Distinguish recreational use from supervised trials. FDA's 2024 refusal of MDMA-for-PTSD approval and emerging research are relevant; require dated update.
- **004: Tattoo, gold, pork.** Explicit Qur'anic prohibitions versus hadith and juristic disagreement.
- **005: Historical practice.** Prophetic and Abbasid/other Golden Ages, wudu/water quality and drinking-water history. The widespread 'everyone drank alcohol because water was undrinkable' tale is geographically/temporally overbroad: https://academic.oup.com/shm/article-abstract/33/3/683/5237763 ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304387824000920
