01 / The recurring architecture

The words that frame a revelation.

The basmala opens 113 of the Qur'an's 114 surahs. Surah 9 is the exception; the phrase appears inside 27:30 as well. That repetition makes divine mercy an important question to study—not a license to ignore justice, obligations, or textual context.

02 / Our operating method

Less inherited certainty. More inspectable evidence.

The Qur'an is our starting authority for this project. Historical reports, tafsir, scholarship, and civilizational experience are valuable evidence—but must be identified as distinct kinds of evidence.

01

Original Arabic first

Inspect the actual wording, morphology, root, surrounding verses, and relevant recitation variations.

02

Separate layers

Distinguish scripture, translation, hadith reports, historical reconstruction, jurisprudence, and present-day opinion.

03

Pressure-test claims

Seek the strongest counter-reading. Mark what is established, disputed, inferred, or unknown.

04

Correct publicly

Publish citations and make revisions traceable. Protect truth from our preferences, too.

03 / The questions worth answering

Start with the highest-leverage disputes.

These are investigations, not predetermined verdicts. Each case will trace the Arabic text, interpretive traditions, historical evidence, and implications for human dignity.

Does the Qur'an require killing apostates?
