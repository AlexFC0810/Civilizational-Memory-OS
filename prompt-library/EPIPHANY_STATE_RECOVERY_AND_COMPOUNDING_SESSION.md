## 4. The Epiphany Ladder — how a future thread must advance
1. **Recall:** what was our last strong idea, and what docs/claims support it?
2. **Correct:** what did we later learn was overstated or false?
3. **Tension:** what is the strongest result that seems to contradict the idea?
4. **Causal bridge:** what mechanism could explain BOTH the positive and negative cases?
5. **Historical test:** retrieve the earliest and strongest records that discriminate among mechanisms.
6. **Synthesis:** what new narrow, transferable claim survives?
7. **Expression:** one evidence-safe visual/story that makes that claim memorable.
8. **Renewal:** what modern institution could embody the result without reproducing historical coercion?
9. **Save delta:** integrate into correct repo; leave one more demanding question than the one we started with.

**Definition of “epiphany”:** a material update to a model of reality backed by something inspectable, not just a novel-sounding slogan. Creative reframes are welcome as *hypotheses*; historical conclusions are earned.
