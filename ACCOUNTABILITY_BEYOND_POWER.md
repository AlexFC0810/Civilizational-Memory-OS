## Core Architecture

Every earthly accountability system has blind spots. The civilizational answer is a triple lock:

1. **Taqwa** — the internal governor.
2. **Institutions** — the external governor.
3. **Anti-idolatry theology** — the interpretive governor.

> **Taqwa governs when the cameras are off. Institutions govern when taqwa is off. Revelation must prevent either one from being captured by the tribe.**
