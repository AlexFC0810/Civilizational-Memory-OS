# Research Programs

This directory holds **governed, multi-case research agendas** that are too broad to be treated as single claim cards and too evidence-dependent to be promoted directly into canon.

A research program converts a high-value question into:

- bounded objects of study;
- shared definitions;
- comparative dimensions;
- evidence requirements;
- breaker evidence;
- priority proof packets;
- promotion gates;
- and repo-routing rules.

> **A research program is not a verdict. It is the machinery required to earn one.**

---

# Relationship to the rest of the repository

- **Canon** defines the governing models and standards.
- **Frameworks** provide reusable analysis protocols.
- **Research programs** define multi-case agendas and proof queues.
- **Research packets and source ledgers** perform the evidence work.
- **Claim cards and evidence matrices** state bounded findings with confidence.
- **Truth and Peace Engine** receives only deployment-safe conclusions with receipts.

No research-program hypothesis should be treated as settled merely because it appears in this directory.

## 3. Abrahamic Revelation, Canon, and Transmission Atlas

File:

- `ABRAHAMIC_REVELATION_CANON_AND_TRANSMISSION_ATLAS.md`

Governing question:

> **What exactly is being compared: revelation, prophetic proclamation, earliest witness, manuscript, textual tradition, canon, interpretation, institution, lived religion, or modern reconstruction?**

Core separations:

- Torah is not a single unchanging historical object in every context;
- Gospel proclamation is not automatically identical to one later codex or canon;
- the Qur'anic Injil must be researched rather than silently equated with the modern New Testament;
- textual alteration, loss, canon selection, interpretive distortion, concealment, and practical betrayal are different hypotheses;
- manuscript preservation does not by itself settle theological interpretation.

Priority queue:

1. Qur'an on previous scripture, verse by verse and category by category.
2. Competing historical models of the Injil.
3. Torah textual plurality.
4. Gospel formation and canon.
5. Qur'anic preservation architecture.
6. Strong and weak forms of Abrahamic continuity.

# Standard promotion path

## Level 0 — Question seed

A potentially valuable pattern or unresolved question.

## Level 1 — Research constitution

Definitions, scope, competing models, evidence requirements, and breaker evidence are explicit.

## Level 2 — Source packets

Primary and specialist evidence is assembled by claim and domain.

## Level 3 — Comparative matrix

Cases are evaluated symmetrically with period-appropriate baselines.

## Level 4 — Defensible synthesis

A bounded verdict survives hostile review and states uncertainty.

## Level 5 — Canon candidate

The finding changes or extends the repository's governing model.

## Level 6 — Deployment-safe export

Safe wording, receipts, likely objections, and downstream routing are complete.

No program may jump from Level 0 or Level 1 directly to public certainty.

- and safe wording.

> **Narrators discover the pattern. Specialists test the pattern. Primary voices reveal the world that generated it. The archive decides what survives.**

# Boundary

Research programs belong here when they are:

- comparative;
- multi-case;
- evidence-intensive;
- not yet canonical verdicts;
- and likely to generate multiple source packets, claim cards, or institutional lessons.

Debate scripts, clips, captions, streamer replies, and campaign assets do not belong here.

> **The goal is not to accumulate impressive questions. It is to build the shortest reliable path from question → evidence → verdict → memory → reimplementation.**
