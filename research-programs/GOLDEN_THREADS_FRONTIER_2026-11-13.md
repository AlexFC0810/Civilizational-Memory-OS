## Ranked P0 — three compounding investigations

### GT-01 / The duty after usefulness — find the animals a society no longer needed

**Current stronger floor:** Islamic hadith/fiqh describes mercy and obligations; ʿIzz al-Dīn ibn ʿAbd al-Salām explicitly said maintenance was owed even to aged/unproductive animals; Ottoman urban rules forbade certain overloading, all cited in the current animal-law packet. **We have enough basis to research law seriously; we do not yet have a generalizable picture of outcomes.**

**New frontier:** Trace a specific provision into actual `ḥisba` inspection, qadi register or animal-waqf deed, recorded expenditure and beneficiary. Did a named fund support a genuinely retired horse, donkey, cat or stork? What was its operating period? Which societies had analogous schemes? Did the rules change as animals lost economic utility?

**Next physical source:** editions and archival catalogues for Ottoman `kanunnāme`, `qadi sijill` and waqf records; existing [animal source gate #112](https://github.com/AlexFC0810/Civilizational-Memory-OS/issues/112) and [older animal issue #110](https://github.com/AlexFC0810/Civilizational-Memory-OS/issues/110). **Stop rule:** no sourced trust deed / court record means no public sanctuary claim.

**Archetype:** The Duty-Bearing Owner / Unheard Creature.  
**Original epiphany:** `An animal's care could become a legal obligation even after it could no longer earn its keep.` (jurist-level until enforcement proved.)  
**Potential asset:** one verified Arabic/Ottoman document + elderly animal; one question in giant type.

### GT-02 / The powerless person's right to object — from imperial abuse to enforceable restraint

**Already known:** ancient historians report Nero's coercive treatment of Sporus; Gaius `Institutes` 1.53 reports Antoninus Pius limiting masters' cruelty within the Roman imperial legal tradition **in the following century**. That proves why a single 'Roman character' archetype is false.

**New frontier:** Across dated Roman, Byzantine, Abbasid and Ottoman contexts, who could actually complain about an elite's cruelty? Which official heard the complaint? Could a slave, minor, non-Muslim, woman, captive or animal's owner/neighbor get protection? Were judgments enforceable against connected elites? Identify actual complaint records, not abstract codes.

**Next primary record:** Gaius 1.52–53 https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Anglica/gai1_Poste.htm ; Roman constitution/provincial petition history; Islamic `maẓālim`/qadi archival records with actual litigants.  
**Archetypes:** Unrestrained Sovereign / Power Conceding a Boundary / Captured Guardian.  
**Potential asset:** a 2-panel Roman internal legal contrast: `NERONIAN COURT — 1st CENTURY` / `ANTONINE LAW — 2nd CENTURY`, then an external period-matched institutional comparison if research supports one.

### GT-03 / The married woman and the court — compare actual legal personhood

**Already known:** Qur'anic female inheritance rules and women's litigation in some Islamic jurisdictions; English medieval coverture restricted married women's independent property disposition, with substantial exceptions for widows, unmarried women and local customs. **The English qualification is itself an important finding, not a rhetorical concession.**

**New frontier:** One **named English married woman** and one **named married woman under Islamic jurisdiction**, in comparable century/status/economic circumstances, with original court disposition. Track complaint, documentary proof, applicable rule, property retained/lost, divorce/widowhood consequences, and execution.

**Source leads:** English National Archives https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/teaching-resources/medieval-women/ ; J. M. Kaye `Women's realty` https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511642241.009 ; Nottingham cases https://doi.org/10.1017/jbr.2019.89 ; Oxford Islamic law literature in existing PWR-02 packet. **Existing owner:** [CMOS #114](https://github.com/AlexFC0810/Civilizational-Memory-OS/issues/114).

**Archetype:** Status-Dependent Legal Person.  
**Potential asset:** `WHAT COULD SHE KEEP WHEN SHE MARRIED?` paired real court documents—not a generic claim that English women had zero rights.

## Ranked P1 — amplifiers with high conceptual range

### GT-04 / Whose 'Dark Ages'? synchronized global maps

**Question:** In a **particular year** (e.g., 900, 1150, 1250), what research, hospitals, trade, education, law and infrastructure were active in Baghdad, Córdoba, Fustat, Constantinople, Latin Europe and Song China? Reconstruct real nodes, don't generalize them from geography.  
**Why:** 'Dark Ages' is a loaded, primarily Eurocentric periodization; even in European historiography it is disputed. An allegedly dark **world** is false, and regional history contains both losses and achievements. **Do not replace one global caricature with a counter-caricature of Europe.**  
**Sources:** Oxford `Towards a Global Middle Ages` https://academic.oup.com/past/article/238/suppl_13/1/5230769 ; `Medieval Studies after the Global Turn` https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/annales-histoire-sciences-sociales-english-edition/article/medieval-studies-after-the-global-turn/6172182D961BBEA9ADD0FF921B94E760 ; existing [Golden Ages Atlas #98](https://github.com/AlexFC0810/Civilizational-Memory-OS/issues/98).  
**Potential asset:** one dated synchronized globe panel `DARK AGE — WHERE?` with sourced actual named institutions.

### GT-05 / Charity becomes public infrastructure — how does mercy outlive the donor?

**Question:** When did a named waqf, hospital or charity carry a source-backed moral aspiration into funded staffing, services, records and patient outcome? Compare same-century Byzantine, Latin Christian and Islamic endowments at **comparable levels** of legal/operational detail.  
**Why:** Institutional durability is more revealing than a solitary story of a generous ruler.  
**Sources:** existing [Golden Ages hospital site #54](https://github.com/AlexFC0810/Civilizational-Memory-OS/issues/54), [CMOS Atlas #98](https://github.com/AlexFC0810/Civilizational-Memory-OS/issues/98), `COMPARATIVE_GOLDEN_AGES_LAB_2026-11-12.md`; Pantokrator typikon and Nur al-Din records.  
**Potential asset:** `THE DONOR DIED. WHY DID THE HOSPITAL STAY OPEN?` followed by archival financing proof.

### GT-06 / Who gets remembered? The unheard source-bias audit

**Question:** How often do our favorite moral heroes come to us from elite chronicles, while the actual ordinary beneficiaries leave court or financial records? What *survives* about slaves, animals, women, children and the defeated—and how can we avoid mistaking archive survivorship for institutional success?  
**Why:** The archive itself filters history; a civilizational memory institution should teach readers how records are produced.  
**Source:** scholarly document methods and established CMOS claim and archive protocol, plus cases PWR-01–PWR-04.  
**Potential asset:** `HISTORY REMEMBERED THE EMPEROR. WHO REMEMBERED THE CHILD?` + named attested source, not invented witness.

## Research priority rubric (1–5 each; NOT civilization ranking)

Rank each opportunity on **(a) demonstrated source depth**, **(b) genuine unresolved novelty**, **(c) causal explanatory range**, **(d) relevance to vulnerable beings**, **(e) feasible original evidence**, and **(f) transferable learning/media value**. Penalize source speculation, side-by-side category mismatch, and production cost. A beautiful, unsourced atrocity or benevolent legend cannot score as "ready" for release.

**P0 recommendation:** Work on GT-01, GT-02 and GT-03 **in parallel as independent source excavations**, not as three agents rewriting the worldview. Each returns an atomic evidence packet (existing claim ID or candidate; exact source; opposing witness; narrow public-safe claim). A **single adjudication/integration seat** updates canon after conflicts are reconciled. The narrative agent can draft review-only storyboards based on the working evidence, but may not certify history.

## Continuous learning loop

`New episode/source -> source-grade finding -> adjust phase archetype -> test on a counterexample -> update the highest-confidence synthesis -> produce one memorable public-safe expression -> log reception and corrections -> update the same record.`

**Definition of compounding:** after a run, the next agent can say "our prior best finding is X, source card Y; my new contribution is Z"—not "here are five fascinating facts about Islam" again.

**Correction policy:** if two sources disagree, preserve both with genre/date, decide the narrowest shared proposition, then escalate only the consequential fork. If a newly found law is real, don't dismiss it as "mere teaching"; if enforcement isn't known, don't assert universal compliance. **Conclusions are required; claims must be graded.**
